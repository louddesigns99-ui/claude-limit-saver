# Contributing

Welcome:
- **Before/after reports**: your week 1 vs week 2 numbers (see docs/measure-it.md). Use the issue template.
- **Rule fixes**: a rule Claude doesn't follow, or one that wastes usage. Say which surface (claude.ai or Claude Code) and model.
- **New procedures** for the skill, if they save real usage and fit in under ~40 lines.

Keep it short. The always-on rules must stay under ~400 words, because they load on every message.
